Course Code: MVJ25EC31

Probability and Transformation Techniques

Equips students with Discrete & Continuous Probability Distributions, Complex Variables, Fourier Series, Fourier & Z-Transforms, and Statistical Curve Fitting essential for DSP, Communications, and AI algorithms.

Type: ASC (4 Credits)
Hours/Week: 4:0:0 (L:T:P)
CIE / SEE: 50 / 50 Marks

Course Outcomes (COs)

  • CO1: Develop probability distributions of discrete, continuous random variables, and joint probability distributions occurring in digital signal processing, information theory, and design engineering.
  • CO2: Understand analytic functions and the application of Bi-linear and Conformal Transformations.
  • CO3: Use periodic signals and Fourier series to analyze circuits and communication systems.
  • CO4: Apply Fourier Transform and Z-Transform techniques to solve difference equations in engineering.
  • CO5: Fit a suitable curve by the method of least squares and determine the lines of regression for statistical data.
